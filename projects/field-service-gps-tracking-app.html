CBA Trackable

As main developer on the current version, I reworked background GPS and offline sync for a Flutter field-service app.

By W.K.D. Kavishka

About the project

Ceylon Business Appliances' field-service app helps staff handle service requests, jobs and claims, and records their route while on duty. I work in Flutter and native Kotlin on the current version.

My contributions

  • Moved GPS recording to an independent Dart isolate with stream recovery, fallback polling, a WorkManager watchdog, and native Android diagnostics for silent service failures.
  • Built a Kotlin activity-recognition plugin and combined motion data with GPS quality checks and parked-mode reporting to avoid sending redundant fixes.
  • Protected unsent field work with ordered offline sync, persistent retries, and isolation of rejected records. Service requests, comments and ownership changes stay usable without a signal.
  • Built an internal journey-analysis viewer with resumable road matching and Docker-based OSRM profiles adapted for Sri Lankan trunk roads.
  • Added background Crashlytics reporting, reduced unnecessary UI rebuilds, and moved image processing off the UI thread.
